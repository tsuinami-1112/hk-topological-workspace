    Read these numbers as

    • Upper bounds. Buildings are not modelled yet, and margins are against LoRa's thermal sensitivity (−134 dBm), not the city's noise floor. The 20 dB threshold is there to absorb that.
    • Weaker direction. Links use the field node's 28 dBm; the home station's 4 W only helps the other way.
    • Antennas. Relays: 6 dBi collinear on a 4 m pole, 1 dB cable. Home: 7 dBi at 47 mPD (13/F), sector 227° to 27°.
    • Sector edge matters. Golden Hill sits 16° inside it. If your clear arc is narrower than 160°, check it.
